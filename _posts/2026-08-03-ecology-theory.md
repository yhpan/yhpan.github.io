## Coordination hypothesis of leaf photosynthesis

> proposed by Chen, Jia-Lin, et al. "Coordination theory of leaf nitrogen distribution in a canopy." \*Oecologia\* 93.1 (1993): 63-69. > 

大致是说植物光合作用模型不以carbon gain最大化去最优化，而是These two processes are defined as Wc, the Rubisco limited rate of carboxylation, and Wj, the electron transport-limited rate of carboxylation. We suggest that plants allocate nitrogen differentially to, leaves in different canopy layers in such a way that Wc and Wj remain roughly balanced.

## Competitor-stress-ruderal theory

植物的生态策略主要由三种基本类型组成——竞争型（C）、耐逆型（S）和扰动型（R），它们分别适应不同的资源、压力和干扰环境。

## resource co-limitation theory
资源共限制理论认为，生物群体的生长和生态过程往往不是由单一资源决定，而是同时受到多个资源的共同限制，例如碳、氮、水分或微量元素的交互作用。 换句话说，生态系统中的生产力和群落结构常常取决于多种资源的协同不足，而不是某一个资源的单独缺乏。
> tested by Anu Eskelinena and Susan P. Harrison. "Resource colimitation governs plant community responses to altered precipitation." \*PNAS\* 112.42 (2015) 13009-13014
> 

Ecological theory and evidence suggest that plant community biomass and composition may often be jointly controlled by climatic water availability and soil nutrient supply.
